What We Do

Pack 95 keeps scouts busy during the school year, with hands-on adventures as a full pack, plus activities tailored to each den's age and skill level.

Pack Adventures

⛺
Camping TripsFall and spring pack campouts under the stars, plus dedicated outings for our older scouts.
🥾
Hikes & Outdoor FunFamily hikes, our First Hike of the year, and plenty of time exploring the outdoors.
🏁
Klondike DerbySled races, winter games, and friendly competition alongside other packs.
🥞
Pancake BreakfastA pack tradition — and a great excuse for seconds.
🎖️
Community ServiceGiving back together, like placing flags on veterans' graves each spring.
🎉
Monthly Pack MeetingsGames, songs, skits, and rank advancement ceremonies for the whole pack.

Fun by Den

K–1stLion & TigerFirst campfires, silly songs, and hands-on crafts that make scouting feel like an adventure from day one.
2ndWolfOutdoor skill-building, team games, and den-led projects that build confidence.
3rdBearCooking over a fire, nature exploration, and bigger badge challenges.
4thWebelosCampout skills, leadership practice, and getting ready for Arrow of Light.
5thArrow of LightJoint outings with local Boy Scout troops, advanced outdoor challenges, and the bridge into Scouts BSA.

This list is a sample of what's planned — let us know if we should tweak or add anything here!

4 How to Join

We welcome scouts in kindergarten through 5th grade year-round. Joining is a three-step process:

1

Register with National (BeAScout)

Sign up with Scouting America and pay national dues — $153, plus optional Scout Life magazine.

Register with National →
2

Register with Pack 95

Fill out our local pack registration form.

Pack Registration Form →
3

Pay Local Pack Dues

Cover local pack activities and supplies for the year. Early Bird Special: $208 · Regular: $275.

Pay Pack Dues →

Questions before you sign up? Email info@sharoncubscouts.com or send us a message on Facebook — we're happy to help.
